Safety Evaluation Methodology

Principles of RouteShield

RouteShield was engineered to present objective, transparent road infrastructure and hazard data to help travelers make informed routing decisions.

Scoring Parameters & Weightings:

  • Street Lighting Density (35%): Evaluates verified lit ways vs. unlit segments derived from municipal open datasets and OpenStreetMap taggings.
  • Pedestrian Infrastructure (25%): Availability of designated sidewalks, separated bike paths, and signalized crosswalks.
  • Active Hazard Deductions (25%): Verified road obstacles, construction zones, or severe lighting defects within a 40m corridor of the route.
  • Data Freshness & Completeness (15%): Measures recency and survey density. Where data is insufficient, an unsupported score is never fabricated.
Transparency Notice: A higher indicator score reflects favorable infrastructural data availability. It is not an assertion of absolute safety. Users must always stay alert and adapt to changing conditions.
